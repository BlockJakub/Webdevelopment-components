FAQ component

A clean question-and-answer pattern for business websites, service pages, and landing pages.

Why use reusable website components?

Reusable components make websites faster to build, easier to maintain, and more consistent across every page.

Is this structure good for search engine optimisation?

Yes. The component uses semantic HTML, clear headings, readable content, and accessible structure.

Can this work without JavaScript?

Yes. The details and summary elements work natively in the browser, so the FAQ opens and closes without JavaScript.
